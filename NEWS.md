# News of Wednesday, 25 November 2026

The six stories on the front page, each checked against news reports
of the day and summarised in my own words.

1. **Two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz (lead).** Iran's
   Revolutionary Guards Navy said two oil tankers struck naval mines
   and caught fire early on Wednesday while crossing the strait.
   The claim itself has not been independently verified.
   Sources: Reuters, 25 Nov 2026; OilPrice, 25 Nov 2026.
2. **AI rally fades as Nvidia sinks.** Nvidia fell about 6% on
   Tuesday after its one-day surge on Jensen Huang's return as CEO
   faded; Asian tech sold off on Wednesday, with Japan's Nikkei
   down 2.8% and the S&P 500 down 0.5%.
   Sources: AP, 25 Nov 2026; Reuters, 25 Nov 2026.
3. **COP31 collapses in Antalya.** The UN climate talks in Antalya
   ended on Sunday 22 November with no overall agreement, after
   oil-producing states blocked any mention of a fossil-fuel
   phase-out and the EU walked out.
   Sources: NPR/KSUT, 22 Nov 2026; CNN, 22 Nov 2026; AP, 23 Nov 2026.
4. **Pakistan's first astronaut reaches Tiangong.** Muhammad Zeeshan
   Ali rode China's Shenzhou-24, launched at 08:18 Beijing time on
   25 November, to the Tiangong space station — the first foreign
   visitor to the station and the first Pakistani in space.
   Sources: Nature, 25 Nov 2026; SpacePolicyOnline, 25 Nov 2026.
5. **All hostages freed in Cartagena siege.** Colombian special
   forces stormed an Éxito supermarket in Cartagena early on
   Wednesday and rescued the last hostages after a siege of about
   17 hours; one gunman was killed and four were detained.
   Sources: AFP via Reuters, 25 Nov 2026; Colombia Reports, 25 Nov 2026.
6. **Senegal wins back Africa Cup title.** The Court of Arbitration
   for Sport overturned the ruling that had handed the trophy to
   Morocco, restoring Senegal as Africa Cup of Nations champions.
   Sources: AP, 25 Nov 2026; NPR, 25 Nov 2026.

Markets strip: Nikkei -2.8%, S&P 500 -0.5%, Brent crude $87.49
(Brent traded between about $87 and $88.70 during 25 November).
Sources: AP, 25 Nov 2026; OneTurn news digest, 25 Nov 2026.
